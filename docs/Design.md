# Sift Design System

Sift is warm, quiet, and premium. Content sits on solid surfaces; Liquid Glass is reserved for the control layer.

## Tokens

- Ink: `#1A1612`
- Ink soft: `#6E6357`
- Ink faint: `#9C9285`
- Bone: `#F6F2EA`
- Card: `#FFFDF8`
- Sand: `#ECE4D6`
- Line: `#E2D9C8`
- Gold: `#B68A4E`
- Gold deep: `#9A7238`
- Clay: `#A8472F`
- Green: `#3F6B3A`

## Typography

- Fraunces for money figures and titles.
- Plus Jakarta Sans for UI and body copy.
- IBM Plex Mono for small uppercase labels, dates, and cadence.

## Geometry

- Cards: 26pt radius.
- Rows: 17pt radius.
- Buttons and fields: 16pt radius.
- Tiles: 11pt radius.
- Pills: 20pt radius.
- Floating glass bars: 24pt radius.

## Glass

Glass belongs on floating tabs, segmented controls, floating action bars, notification banners, and sheets. Never put body text directly on glass, and never stack glass on glass.

Motion and glass behavior are specified in `13-MOTION-AND-GLASS.md`.
