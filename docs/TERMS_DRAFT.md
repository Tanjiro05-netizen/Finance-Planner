## The service

Sift is an iPhone app that reads your Apple Card, Apple Cash and Apple Pay activity — with your permission, through Apple's FinanceKit — and helps you understand it: finding recurring subscriptions, tracking budgets and savings goals, and estimating what you can safely spend before your next payday.

Sift runs on your device. There is no Sift account and no Sift server.

## Not financial advice

Sift is an organisation and budgeting tool. It does not provide financial, investment, tax, or legal advice, and it does not recommend financial products. Every figure it shows is a calculation from your own data, presented to help you decide for yourself.

Where Sift generates written summaries or answers questions, those are produced by an on-device language model working from figures Sift has already calculated. They may be worded imperfectly. Treat them as a description of your numbers, not as advice, and check anything that matters against the figures themselves.

## Accuracy and detection

Sift identifies subscriptions, bills and income using pattern detection, which is a best effort and not guaranteed. It may miss recurring charges, flag one-off purchases as recurring, or mis-categorise spending.

Balances, forecasts, and "safe to spend" figures are estimates based on the data available on your device at that moment. They are not a statement of your actual account balance and should not be relied on as one. Your bank is the authority on your money.

You can correct what Sift gets wrong: bills, income, categories, budgets and goals can all be added and edited by hand.

## Cancelling subscriptions

**There is no universal cancel button, and Sift does not have one.** No app can cancel a subscription on your behalf without contacting the provider.

Sift shows you the real cancellation steps for a provider, and can remind you before a renewal. Completing a cancellation is between you and the provider, and Sift cannot guarantee that a provider will accept or complete one.

Concierge cancellation — where a person acts on your behalf — is described in the app but is not currently offered. If it is ever enabled it will require an account and additional terms.

## Your responsibilities

- Keep your device secure. Because your data lives on your iPhone, your device passcode and Face ID are what protect it.
- Verify anything financially significant against your bank and the provider directly.
- Confirm with the provider whether a cancellation actually completed.

## Acceptable use

Do not attempt to reverse engineer, tamper with, or misuse the app, or use it to break the law or a third party's terms.

## Availability and changes

Sift may change, suspend, or discontinue features. Because the app runs on your device, most functionality does not depend on us being online — but features that rely on Apple services (FinanceKit availability, Apple Intelligence, notifications) depend on those services and on your device supporting them.

Some features are limited by region and hardware. FinanceKit access is available only on iPhone in supported countries; on-device AI features require a device that supports Apple Intelligence.

## Notes for counsel — remove before publishing

- Sift holds no user data and operates no server; there is no account to terminate and no data to return or delete on request. Termination clauses drafted for SaaS will not fit and may be actively misleading here.
- The "no financial advice" and "estimates, not balances" sections are the two most important protections given what the app displays. The safe-to-spend figure is prominent and could be relied on.
- The AI disclosure is deliberate: the model is instructed never to calculate figures itself, only to phrase figures Sift computed. That instruction is enforced by prompt and by construction, not by a guarantee, so the wording above does not promise correctness.
- Concierge cancellation is currently disabled both by feature flag and by the on-device client reporting that it cannot service it. If enabled, revisit this document and the privacy policy together.
