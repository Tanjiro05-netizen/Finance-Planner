# Sift Terms Of Service Draft

Draft for counsel review. This document is not legal advice and must not be published until reviewed and approved by qualified counsel.

## Service

Sift provides tools to identify recurring subscription charges from read-only financial data and to help users manage cancellation steps. Guided cancellation provides instructions and reminders. Concierge cancellation, when enabled, creates a request for Sift's team to help pursue a cancellation path.

## No Financial Advice

Sift is an organization and productivity tool. It does not provide financial, legal, tax, or investment advice. Users are responsible for their own subscription, banking, and merchant account decisions.

## Plaid And Financial Data

Sift uses Plaid to connect to financial institutions. Users authorize Plaid Link directly. Sift does not collect bank credentials. Plaid access tokens are handled only by the backend and are encrypted at rest.

## Cancellation Limits

Sift does not provide a universal cancellation API and does not guarantee that a merchant will accept or complete a cancellation. Some providers require the account holder to complete steps directly. If concierge operations are not enabled, Sift presents concierge as coming soon and provides guided cancellation only.

## User Responsibilities

Users must provide accurate information, keep their account secure, follow merchant cancellation terms, and confirm whether a cancellation has been completed by the provider.

## Acceptable Use

Users may not misuse the service, attempt unauthorized access, submit false information, interfere with backend systems, or use Sift to violate law or third-party terms.

## Availability

Sift may change, suspend, or discontinue parts of the service. Backend, Plaid, bank, notification, or merchant outages may affect availability.
